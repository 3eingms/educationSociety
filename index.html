About Shadab Education

Shadab Education Society, established in 2010 in Kolhapur, is a dedicated NGO committed to providing free and affordable education to children who cannot afford school fees. With a special focus on early childhood learning, we currently offer quality education to LKG, UKG, and Nursery students, nurturing young minds with care, values, and strong academic foundations. Driven by the belief that every child deserves the right to education, we aim to expand our reach in the future and empower more underprivileged children with opportunities to learn, grow, and build a brighter tomorrow

The Visionary Behind Our School

Founder - Shabbir Ahmed Abdul Aziz Sayyed

Born 23/11/1954 , Death 01/05/2020

In the name of Allah, Most Gracious, Most Merciful Golden memories! Dear teacher, God has endowed everyone with unique attributes to achieve their life’s deeds. I am privileged to write about the beloved Syed Shabir Sir of Pune. Our families have been close for years as neighbors and friends. Sir always helped everyone and greeted children with a smile. I studied at his school, where his kindness was exemplary. Later, as a trainee teacher at his college, I saw many facets of his ideal personality. Syed Sir taught his students to uphold spiritual and moral values, develop a scientific outlook, and become enlightened yet traditional individuals. His mission was to create ideal teachers who emphasized character building and punctuality. Though his students sometimes complained, they later realized the value of his strict guidance in their practical lives.

Why SHADAB EDUCATION SOCIETY

Shadab Education Society is committed to breaking the cycle of poverty through education, ensuring every child gets a strong foundation for a brighter future.

Free & Affordable Early Education

Providing structured Nursery, LKG, and UKG education to underprivileged children who cannot afford school fees.

Holistic Learning Approach

Focusing on academic basics along with values, moral education, and social development from an early age.

Community Support Programs

Engaging parents and local communities to raise awareness about the importance of early childhood education.

Access to Learning Resources

Offering books, creative tools, and interactive teaching methods to nurture curiosity and confidence in children

Future Expansion Plans

Working towards expanding our reach to more children and building better infrastructure for long-term impact.
